About Me

I am a passionate and versatile Full Stack Developer with a strong foundation in modern web and mobile technologies, coupled with a keen interest in cutting-edge fields like Agentic AI and Blockchain. My expertise spans across intuitive front-end development using Flutter, React, React Native, Next.js, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript, ensuring dynamic and responsive user experiences.

On the back-end, I leverage Node.js and Express.js to build robust and scalable server-side applications, seamlessly integrated with MongoDB for efficient data management. I am adept at version control with Git & GitHub, ensuring collaborative and streamlined development workflows.

Beyond core development, I enhance user interfaces with libraries like GSAP for captivating animations and style applications with frameworks such as Bootstrap, Tailwind CSS, and Daisy UI. My foundational knowledge in C, Java, and Python further strengthens my problem-solving abilities, while my active exploration of Agentic AI and Blockchain positions me at the forefront of future technological innovation.

I am driven by a desire to create impactful solutions and continuously learn and adapt in the ever-evolving tech landscape.
